Status legend (SmartHub Desktop + ADB):

Strict AI rule:
- Only say "No problems detected" when the scan has no failing stages and no suspicious app/security signals.

Rule: If an item is not marked ✅ or ⚠, treat it as 🕒 for now.

✅ Why Dialer Codes Cannot Do These
Dialer tests are static, manufacturer‑specific, and provide no fixes.

They cannot modify system settings, clear caches, or interact with running apps.

They lack permission to access battery statistics, app behavior logs, or perform network stack resets.

No app analysis, Bluetooth management, or security scanning.

A proper diagnostic app, even running on the device with standard permissions (or via ADB on a PC), can actively repair, reconfigure, and report – turning diagnosis into action.
