1. Purpose
This app is designed to work together with the SmartHub desktop tool. It collects technical information from this phone (such as battery status, storage usage, basic hardware information and logs) to help technicians diagnose problems.

2. Data collected
- Battery level and health information
- Storage capacity and free space
- Basic device identifiers (model, manufacturer, Android version, build fingerprint)
